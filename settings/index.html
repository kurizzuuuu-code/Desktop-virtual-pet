Custom speech bubbles

One line per message — a random line is picked each time. Use {name} for your pet's name. Style with **bold**, *italic*, __underline__, ~~strike~~. Leave blank to use built-in defaults. Sounds and voices for these lines live in the Voice tab.

AI brain (optional)

Chat answers come from her built-in brain by default. Add an API key to upgrade her to real AI for tricky questions (boss guides, builds). Free tier: Google AI Studio, model gemini-2.5-flash. Only manual chat messages use it — everything else stays free and offline.

Task mode (> build me a website) runs on your provider chain — best local with Ollama + qwen2.5:7b. Reads run free; created/edited/recycled files and every terminal command ask first. Shell runs PowerShell, non-interactive, 60s per command.

How she notices games

Three ways, simplest first: window titles (automatic) → running processes → screen reading / log files (most setup). You only need one.

React to what you're doing

Built into the app (Windows API) — no admin, no extra permissions. Click Test, then within 3 seconds focus YouTube or your game.

Detect games without log files

Minecraft bot

She joins Java Edition as a real player: follows you, wanders nearby, looks around, wears armor she finds, sleeps at night, and answers chat with her personality. Orders: come / stay / follow <name> / jump / guard / get wood [n] / get food [n] / right click / left click / look here / look there / look at 123 65 456. Right click uses whatever she's holding or looking at (places a block, opens a chest, eats, uses an item); left click attacks whatever's in front of her (or swings at the block). She hits back whoever hits her (mobs, bosses, even players — hit for hit). LAN worlds (open to LAN) and offline-mode servers just need a name; online-mode servers need Microsoft login — first connect shows a code to enter at microsoft.com/link.
